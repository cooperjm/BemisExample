Fit Guide

Compatibility Studio

Ensure a flawless fit. Follow our interactive checkpoints to verify layout compatibility with your bathroom.

Is your toilet Round or Elongated?

Measure from the center of the bolt holes to the very front edge of the toilet bowl.

  • Elongated (Fits)18.5" or longer
  • Round (Out of Stock)16.5" to 17.5"

Note: Round style is currently unavailable. Elongated fixtures are fully supported.

One-Piece vs. Two-Piece Compatibility

The BB-500 fits all standard two-piece toilets and most modern one-piece toilets.

Fits Standard flat bowls, French curve bowls with 2"+ clearance
May Not Fit Toilets with extreme curvature or integrated rear control tanks

GFCI Outlet Required

To power the heated seat, water warmer, and electronic controls, you will need a 120V GFCI outlet located within 4 feet of the toilet bowl.

Cord Specifications

The standard power cord is 4 feet long and extends from the right-hand side of the unit (facing the toilet).
